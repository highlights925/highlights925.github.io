## About

Qi Sun received his MEng in Cyberspace Security from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (2025) and his BSc in Data Science from North University of China (2022). He works on knowledge-augmented root-cause analysis, multivariate time series anomaly detection, and safety verification for cyber-physical systems.
