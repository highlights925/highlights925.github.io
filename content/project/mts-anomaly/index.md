## Motivation

Catalytic cracking and other process units exhibit strong variable couplings. Local anomalies can cascade if not detected early.

## Approach

- Spatial-temporal dependency models for multivariate process series (**ICIC 2024**).
- Diagonal state space autoencoders (**DS2AE**) for detection (**CCC 2025**).
- Causal tracing (MIC-TDTE) for root-cause diagnosis (**CAC 2024**).

## Outcomes

A family of methods spanning detection, classification of failure modes, and causal diagnosis on TEP / CCP-style industrial datasets.
