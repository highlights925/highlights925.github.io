## Study focus

In catalytic-cracking regeneration, coupled process variables can change together, making a detected deviation different from its initiating cause. This paper combines detection with causal tracing so that diagnosis can move from “what is abnormal?” toward “where did it begin, and how did it propagate?”

## Method

A diagonal state-space autoencoder (DS2AE) detects abnormal process variables. The causal model uses the maximum information coefficient (MIC) and time-delayed transfer entropy (TDTE) to estimate relationships among variables. The resulting graph is traversed to trace propagation paths and identify candidate root causes.

## Evaluation

The method is evaluated on abnormal operating-condition data generated by a catalytic-cracking process simulation system. The paper reports that the combined detector and causal-tracing workflow is effective on this case study; the abstract does not state a numeric score, so none is inferred here.
