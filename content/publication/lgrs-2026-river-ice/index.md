abstract: Accurate segmentation of river ice in remote sensing imagery remains challenging due to heterogeneous ice textures, fragmented floes, and ambiguous ice–water boundaries. While DeepLabv3+ effectively captures multi-scale context, its standard atrous convolution suffers from uniform sampling and gridding effects, which limit boundary discrimination in complex river ice scenes. To address these issues, we propose a lightweight enhancement to DeepLabv3+ that integrates multiscale sparse-aware dilated convolution and a boundary-interactive ASPP module.
