abstract: Advanced driver assistance system (ADAS) involves discrete function switches with continuous dynamics, thus sudden changes of different control strategies may lead vehicles to lose stability and safety. Existing analysis mainly focuses on individual functions and often lacks interpretable control procedures with safety guarantees. This paper proposes an interpretable vehicular safety control approach based on hybrid automata and deep reinforcement learning.
