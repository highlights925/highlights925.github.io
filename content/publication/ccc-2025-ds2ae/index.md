abstract: Detecting anomalies in multivariate time series derived from chemical process variables is a critical approach to ensuring operational safety, as failures in detection may result in the cascading propagation of local anomalies, potentially leading to major accidents. In the catalytic cracking reaction regeneration process, complex coupling relationships exist among variables, making it difficult for traditional models to accurately capture the spatiotemporal dependencies inherent in such data. This paper proposes a DS2AE-based anomaly detection method for the reaction-regeneration process in catalytic cracking units.
